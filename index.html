About

I am a graduate student in the M.S.–Ph.D. integrated program at the Department of Artificial Intelligence, Hanyang University. My research centers on reinforcement learning, model-based RL, world models, and robotics AI.

I am broadly interested in energy-efficient autonomous robot control and safe, robust decision-making in real-world environments. My current work includes energy-optimal locomotion for quadruped robots that adapts to changing ground friction, energy-aware autonomous navigation for mobile robots, and physics-constrained reinforcement learning with sim-to-real transfer.

In the area of medical microrobotics, I study CT/X-ray-based 3D environment perception, autonomous intravascular navigation, and safe autonomous control that combines world-model-based planning with reinforcement learning.
